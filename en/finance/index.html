Finance is the study of how individuals and organisations manage money, assets and risk over time. In this encyclopedia, the subject refers to the author's study of financial markets, with emphasis on long-term wealth building and the understanding of risk.

Overview

Investments are commonly divided into two broad groups according to how their returns are determined. This distinction helps an investor understand the level of risk and the time horizon of each option.

This article is educational and does not constitute investment advice.

Areas of study

  • Fixed income
    • Government bonds: debt securities issued by the national treasury, with rules for remuneration defined at the time of purchase.
    • Bank securities: debt instruments issued by financial institutions, such as certificates of deposit.
  • Variable income
    • Stocks: ownership shares in a company, whose prices fluctuate with the market.
    • Dividends: portions of a company's profit distributed to its shareholders.
    • Futures: contracts to buy or sell an asset at a set price on a future date.

Study approach

Study begins with core concepts, such as risk, liquidity and the time value of money, before specific instruments are analysed. Diversification reduces exposure to the risk of any single asset. Reading a company's financial statements supports a more informed evaluation of its shares.
