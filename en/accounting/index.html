Accounting is the systematic recording, classification and reporting of financial transactions. In this encyclopedia, the subject refers to the author's study of accounting in preparation for a public service examination in Brazil.

Overview

Accounting records transactions in a ledger, a structured book of accounts, and summarises them in financial statements. These statements give managers, investors and public authorities a reliable picture of an organisation's financial position.

Areas of study

  • Financial accounting: the preparation of financial statements, such as the balance sheet and the income statement.
  • Cost accounting: the measurement and analysis of the costs of producing goods or services.
  • Public sector accounting: the recording and reporting of the finances of government bodies, with emphasis on legal compliance and transparency.
  • Auditing: the independent examination of financial records to verify that they are accurate and comply with applicable rules.

Study approach

Study prioritises the topics that appear most often in past examinations. Solving previous exam questions under timed conditions builds speed and reveals gaps in understanding. Core concepts are learned first, and specialised rules are added afterwards.
