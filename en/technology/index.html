Technology is the application of scientific knowledge to solve practical problems. In this encyclopedia, the subject refers to the author's technical interests, which combine software, electronic hardware and systems that operate with little supervision.

Overview

Software, electronics and automation are closely connected. A program written in JavaScript or another language can read a sensor, control a motor or send a notification, which links the digital and physical worlds.

Areas of interest

  • Programming: the writing of instructions that a computer can execute, with emphasis on web development and clean code.
  • Electronics: the design and repair of circuits built from components such as resistors, capacitors and microcontrollers.
  • Automation: the use of technology to perform tasks with minimal human intervention, in homes and in industry.
  • Robotics: the construction of machines that sense their environment and act on it.

Study approach

Hands-on projects, such as assembling a circuit or automating a simple task, consolidate theory better than reading alone. A working prototype, even a simple one, reveals problems that planning alone does not. The approach also supports self-reliance, since learned skills reduce the need to hire third-party services.

See also
