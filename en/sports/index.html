Sport is a form of physical activity governed by rules and practised for recreation, competition or health. In this encyclopedia, the subject refers to the author's sports and fitness activities, which combine team play with individual training.

Overview

Regular physical activity supports health, discipline and mental clarity. Team sports add cooperation and communication, while individual training allows progress to be measured and adjusted independently.

Activities

  • Team sports
    • Football: a team game that combines endurance with passing and positioning.
    • Basketball: a fast-paced team game that develops agility and coordination.
  • Racket sports
    • Beach tennis: a racket sport played on sand, similar to tennis but with a lower net and solid rackets.
  • Individual training
    • Calisthenics: strength training that uses bodyweight exercises such as push-ups and pull-ups.
    • Jump rope: a low-cost cardiovascular exercise that improves timing and foot speed.

Training approach

Regular sessions improve endurance, strength and coordination. Gradual progression, adequate rest and a varied routine reduce the risk of injury and help maintain motivation.

See also

  • Music: jump rope and drumming both rely on rhythm and coordination.
  • Technology: sensors and wearable devices can record training data.
