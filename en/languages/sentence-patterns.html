This article is part of the Languages category.

Sentence patterns
CategoryLanguage learning

LanguagesEnglish, with Portuguese translations
RelatedLanguages, vocabulary

A sentence pattern is a reusable frame in which fixed words give the structure and letters such as X, Y and Z mark the places where new words are inserted. Learning the frames of encyclopedic writing allows many correct sentences to be built from a few models. The patterns are ordered by priority: the essential ones are the few that appear most often and give the greatest return for the time spent.
