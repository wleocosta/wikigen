Music is the art of organising sound in time. In this encyclopedia, the subject refers specifically to the author's instrumental practice across four instruments, each of which contributes a different role within an ensemble.

Overview

The author studies harmony, rhythm and technique through regular practice. Learning several instruments provides a broader understanding of how melody, harmony and rhythm interact.

Instruments

  • Acoustic guitar: a stringed instrument used for chords and accompaniment.
  • Bass guitar: provides the harmonic foundation and rhythmic pulse.
  • Keyboard: allows chords, melodies and arrangements to be visualised.
  • Drums: define tempo, groove and dynamics.

Practice approach

Consistent, deliberate practice yields faster progress than unstructured repetition. Short daily sessions focused on a single objective are preferred over long, irregular ones.
